Principles & patterns

Colour means intensity

Saturated hues are reserved for zones. The shell stays neutral so a glance reads effort, not decoration.

Calm by default

Warm paper, generous space, hairline borders over heavy shadows. Nothing competes for attention.

Numbers are tabular

Durations, watts, TSS and dates align in columns and never jitter as values change.

One accent, used rarely

Sage marks the active place and brand moments only — so it stays meaningful.

Built for the thumb

On mobile, tap targets stay ≥44px and the live session view shows one step at a time.

Athlete chooses the depth

Optional overview cards (coach note, activity, trends) are opt-in from Settings — not forced on everyone.
