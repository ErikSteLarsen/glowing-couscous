# Impeccable critique: coach Workouts + Templates + Planner/Builder

Method: dual-agent (A: source design review · B: detector/source evidence)

## Scope

Coach Workouts, Templates, Planner/Source Assignment, Planner/Builder boundary,
their directly used clients, and shared editor/action seams.

## Evidence before the batch

- Assessment A scored the family **19/40** against the week-at-a-glance goal.
- Assessment B detector returned `[]` for 17 targeted route/client/shared files.
- Browser viewport/runtime evidence was unavailable.

## Highest-value findings addressed

1. Main Planner athlete and Source Assignment athlete could diverge.
2. Assignment success left the visible week stale and did not identify the target.
3. Planner/source network failures could leave actions stuck or silent.
4. Planner delete lacked confirmation; day sessions after the first two were hidden.
5. Planner and assignment feedback were not consistently announced.

## Deferred

- Cadence-specific target controls and explicit month guided setup.
- Full week-wall target-selector redesign and visible week navigation.
- Keyboard block reordering and Builder capability expansion.

## Browser limitation

No connected desktop browser was available; screenshots, computed styles,
keyboard walkthroughs, and touch inspection remain required.
