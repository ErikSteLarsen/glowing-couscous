Method: dual-agent (A: source design review · B: detector/source evidence)

Athlete Planner, athlete Templates, coach discovery, directly used template/
editor/share clients, and their state/read seams.

## Evidence before the batch

- Assessment A scored the family **20/40** against the week-at-a-glance goal.

4. Coach discovery search/request states lacked labels, announcements, and clear pending continuation.
