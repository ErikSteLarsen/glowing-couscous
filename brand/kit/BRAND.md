# Wattonaut brand kit

The symbol is a planet with a mountain ridge. The ridge reads as a W and rises to the right.

- `logo/`: `mark`, `mark-small` (16-32 px), `wordmark`, `logo`, `stacked` and `monogram` (WN), each for
  `dark` and `light` backgrounds and in `black` and `white`. Text is outlined. PNG copies are in `png/`.
- `web/`: browser, Apple and Android icons and the share image. `social/`: avatar and banner.
- Colors: dark uses sky `#1C7BA2` and ground `#5EB6D8`; light uses `#0F4A64` and `#1C7BA2`.
- Lettering: Instrument Sans, "watt" 600 and "onaut" 500, lowercase in the logo. Write Wattonaut in text.
- Clear space: a quarter of the symbol on every side. Do not stretch, rotate, recolor or add effects.

Trademark registers and similar logos have not been checked.

`build.py` regenerates everything, including the app icons, desktop icons and `components/BrandMark.tsx`.
